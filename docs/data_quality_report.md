## Caveats

- **Synthetic data.** CMS synthesis weakens real relationships between variables; results demonstrate method, not clinical truth.
- **Top-coded costs.** Payments are rounded and capped by CMS, so the true high-cost tail is hidden. High-cost flags use Tukey fences on log(cost), not mean ± standard deviation; the extreme fence sits above the CMS cap, so no extreme outliers survive top-coding.
- **Orphan segments** have no date, so yearly totals sum to slightly less than the all-time total (see `ORPHAN_SEGMENT_OUTPATIENT`).
- **No carrier (physician) claims** are included, so cost per member is understated.
